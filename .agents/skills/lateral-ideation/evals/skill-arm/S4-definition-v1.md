SCAMPER is a checklist of seven ways to change an existing thing:

- **S**ubstitute: swap a part, material, person or step for something else.
- **C**ombine: merge it with something that does a different job.
- **A**dapt: borrow a mechanism from a field that already solved a similar problem.
- **M**odify: change a property. Often split into Magnify and Minify.
- **P**ut to another use: same thing, new job or new user.
- **E**liminate: remove the part everyone treats as essential.
- **R**everse (or Rearrange): flip the order, the direction, or who does what.

Bob Eberle published it in 1971, built on Alex Osborn's idea-spurring questions.
