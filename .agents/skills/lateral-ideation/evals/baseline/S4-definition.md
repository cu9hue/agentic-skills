SCAMPER is a checklist of seven prompts for changing an existing product, process, or idea:

- **S – Substitute.** Swap a part, material, person, or step for something else.
- **C – Combine.** Merge two ideas, features, or functions into one.
- **A – Adapt.** Borrow something that works elsewhere and fit it to your case.
- **M – Modify.** Change size, shape, or other attributes. Some versions use Magnify or Minify here.
- **P – Put to another use.** Use it for a different purpose or a different user.
- **E – Eliminate.** Remove a part or simplify it down to the core.
- **R – Reverse.** Flip the order or turn it inside out. Some versions use Rearrange.

Bob Eberle built it in 1971 from Alex Osborn's brainstorming questions.
