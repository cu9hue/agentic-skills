**"Cut a vital connection."**

For an intro, try it like this. Find the sentence your intro feels like it can't do without. It's usually the thesis statement or the "In this essay I will..." line. Delete it. Then start with whatever comes next: a scene, a number, a question, the strangest claim you make anywhere in the essay.

Most stuck intros are stuck because they explain the essay before they start it. Remove the explaining and see whether the reader still knows where they are. Usually they do.

If that doesn't move you, draw again: **"Work at a different speed."** Give yourself four minutes to write the worst possible intro, with no edits. Then keep the one line that surprised you.
