**What to get right, if this is your first campaign**

- Lead with what you want to learn, not with a question about their setup. The sharp questions go on the call.
- Ask for 15 minutes of advice. Never pitch, and never promise insights you don't have.
- Send in batches of 10–20, Monday to Wednesday. Follow up after about 3 business days. If a batch gets zero replies, change the message before you spend more prospects.
- Skip open tracking. Track replies by hand, and compare reply rates by personalization after a few batches.
