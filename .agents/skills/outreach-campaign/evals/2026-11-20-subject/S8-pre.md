## How to send

You send everything by hand. I draft and track only.

1. **LinkedIn request with no note** to Maya and Dev. Set Status to `Request sent`.
2. **Sara starts on email**, because she has no LinkedIn URL. First send her draft to your own Gmail and Outlook accounts and see where it lands. Then send it as plain text with no link and no open tracking. Set Status to `Sent` and fill Sent.
3. **Message 1 within minutes of an acceptance.** Set Status to `Accepted` when they accept, then `Sent` and fill Sent when the message is out.
4. **Dev's email** about 5 business days after the request, only if he has not accepted. Maya gets no email.
5. **The nudge** when they answer the question. Status goes `Replied`, then `Followed up`.
6. **Two times or the calendar link** within the hour of a yes. Status `Call booked`. The link appears nowhere before this reply.
7. **One follow-up** about 3 business days after message 1 with no answer. Follow-up due shows the date. Then stop.

What to get right:

- Send requests Monday to Wednesday. Next week is Thanksgiving week in the US (26 November). Send on Monday or Tuesday, or hold the batch until 30 November.
- Keep your LinkedIn profile current. The messages carry no background sentence, so the profile is the prospect's first look at you.
- Finish each draft by hand but keep it to three sentences. No apology, no background, no link, no pitch, no promise to share findings.
- Do not add commas or colons back. Tidy, identical text reads as a template to a person and to a spam filter.
- Mark a bounce `invalid`.
- Track acceptance apart from replies. Low acceptance is a list or profile problem. Low replies after acceptance is a message problem.
- Count calls per person approached, by First touch. LinkedIn-first has booked calls so far; email has not yet.
- Three people is a small batch. Expect 5 to 10% of people approached to reply, so 5 calls takes roughly 50 to 100 people. A normal pace is 15 to 20 requests a day.
- If a batch gets zero replies, change one thing in the message before you spend more prospects.
- End each call with a small commitment question, for example "Could I look at one slice of your claims data under an NDA?" Log the answer under Commitment in Calls.
