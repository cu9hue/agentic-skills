What to get right:

- Finish each draft by hand. Keep it to three sentences. No apology, no background, no link.
- The message carries no background sentence. Your LinkedIn profile does that job, so make sure it is current.
- Track acceptance apart from replies. A low acceptance rate is a list or profile problem. A low reply rate after acceptance is a message problem.
- Mark a bounce `invalid`. Do not use open tracking; track by hand in Notion.
- Count calls per person approached, by First touch. LinkedIn first has booked calls so far; email has not yet.
- Expect about 5 to 10% of people approached to reply. Three people will not give you a call on their own. Five calls take roughly 50 to 100 people, at 15 to 20 requests a day.
- If a batch gets zero replies, change one thing in the message before you spend more good prospects.
- Never pitch and never promise insights you do not have. End each call with the small commitment question.
