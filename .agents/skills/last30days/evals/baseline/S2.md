**Short version:** Granola gets the warmest user talk, Fireflies splits opinion, and Otter collects most of the complaints. But this month the three are not mostly being compared with each other. The story is Anthropic's **Claude Meetings** (beta from Nov 12) and the wave of "Anthropic just killed Otter, Granola, Fireflies" posts that followed it. All three vendors answered by moving past the note itself: Granola toward agents, Fireflies toward an all-in-one "AI teammate", Otter toward languages.

## The big story: Claude Meetings

- Anthropic shipped [Claude Meetings](https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-meetings) in beta on Nov 12. Claude joins a call as a visible participant, writes notes and action items, and builds docs and whiteboards during the call. Notes and transcripts are free during the beta.
- X filled up with "Anthropic just killed…" lists. The biggest one I found had ~17k views and named Otter, Granola, Fireflies and about 25 others ([example](https://x.com/0x0SojalSec/status/2125338912071815368)).
- The pushback is reasonable. The beta works on **Google Meet only**, on **Team and Enterprise plans only**. On Enterprise it stays out of calls with outside guests by default. It also treats a client's Fireflies or Otter bot as an outsider and leaves ([Zach Senkoji](https://x.com/senkoji/status/2125606811503907205), [Ryan Doser](https://ryandoser.com/best-ai-note-takers/)). It keeps no audio. Auto-join had an outage from Nov 16 to 19 ([ClaudeCowork](https://www.claudecowork.com/comparisons/claude-meetings-vs-fireflies/)).
- Anthropic lists Granola, Fireflies and Otter as partners that will receive Claude Meetings transcripts. The analyst read is that the note is becoming free, so a notetaker now has to sell what happens after the note ([AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.ca/ai-in-business/claude-meetings-notetaker-economics-canada/), [State of AI](https://www.thestateofai.com/news/anthropic-claude-meetings-ai-notetakers)). Gartner calls it "a beta entrant in a mature market" ([Computerworld](https://www.computerworld.com/article/4247466/anthropic-targets-workplace-collaboration-with-claude-meetings.html)).

## Granola

**What people like:** no bot joins the call. That is still the most praised feature in the whole category. One tweet sums up the mood: "granola is muscle memory atp." Users also say its notes beat the summaries built into Zoom, Teams and Copilot.

**What people dislike:** it is weak at labelling who said what, and it keeps no audio to replay. One founder calls it "a simple notetaker" that wins on brand and design.

**New this month:**
- Agent Briefs (Nov 11): a nightly summary of your work that Claude or ChatGPT reads first. Business and Enterprise plans only ([Granola](https://www.granola.ai/blog/introducing-your-agent-brief)).
- MCP 2.0 (Nov 12): transcript search that Granola says uses 85% fewer tokens ([Granola](https://www.granola.ai/blog/how-we-help-your-agent-find-what-its-looking-for)).
- Free voice typing on Android.
- Granola says more than 1M of its notes are read by agents every day ([@meetgranola](https://x.com/DJVagina/status/2123818720044749186)).

A typical user story: one person ran the same speakerphone call through Granola and Wispr Flow's new iPhone notetaker. Granola caught every word, and Claude sent the follow-up through MCP. Wispr caught nothing ([Matt Hebard](https://x.com/HebardMatt/status/2123450080455708862)).

**Watch out:** "no bot" is not a legal shield. A class action filed in July (*Chamberlain v. Granola*) alleges that the notices to other participants were off by default ([Sally](https://www.sally.io/blog/ai-notetaker-lawsuits)).

## Fireflies

**What people like:** integrations, CRM push and search across past meetings. Heavy meeting users say it gives them hours back ("cut about 3 hours of manual work a week").

**What people dislike:** the bot. "Half my team stopped using Fireflies because clients weren't comfortable" is a quote that gets repeated. Some users also report wrong AI notes and bugs.

**New this month:** a large relaunch on Nov 11 as "Fred, an AI teammate". It covers notes, email, Slack, scheduling, enterprise search, and a first look at a hardware device, Fireflies Fly ([press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/11/11/3400748/0/en/fireflies-ai-evolves-from-ai-notetaker-to-ai-teammate-in-its-most-significant-expansion-to-date.html)). Fireflies Talk, its free dictation feature, now does voice typing inside other apps on iPhone and made the top 5 on a dictation benchmark. Most of the Fireflies posts on X this month come from Fireflies itself.

**Watch out:** voiceprint (BIPA) lawsuits are pending in Illinois and California.

## Otter

**What people like:** accurate live transcripts. Otter also posted the best word error rate in an independent test this month (see below).

**What people dislike:** behaviour in other people's meetings. Users say it auto-joins calls, emails invitees to sign up and is hard to remove. They also mention minute caps and weak summaries. In one Reddit tally Otter has **5 positive and 25 negative** voices ([MetaWhisp](https://metawhisp.com/blog/best-ai-note-taker-reddit/)).

**New this month:** 27 new languages plus transcription that follows speakers who switch languages mid-meeting (Nov 10, [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/09/otter-ai-can-now-follow-conversations-across-30-languages/)). There is also a Black Friday deal: 50% off annual Pro and Business plans through Nov 29.

**Watch out:** the consent class action (*In re Otter.AI Privacy Litigation*) is in discovery. The core wiretap and biometric claims survived in August, and class certification is due in May 2027 ([Consentra](https://consentra.co/blog/otter-ai-lawsuit-explained)).

Sources: [Meeting Tools Lab accuracy test](https://meetingtoolslab.com/articles/ai-note-takers-accuracy-test/), [MetaWhisp](https://metawhisp.com/blog/best-ai-note-taker-reddit/), [GTM Ranked](https://gtmranked.com/best/conversation-intelligence/), [Ryan Doser pricing table](https://ryandoser.com/best-ai-note-takers/). The accuracy gaps are small enough that a different recording could change the order.

## So which one?

- **Client calls on any platform, no bot:** Granola. Tell people you are taking notes.
- **Sales or ops team that wants notes pushed into the CRM and a searchable archive:** Fireflies.
- **Live transcript, many languages, or the Black Friday price:** Otter. Turn off auto-join and auto-share.
- **Internal Google Meet calls and you already pay for Claude Team or Enterprise:** try Claude Meetings first. It is free during the beta.
