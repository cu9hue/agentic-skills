# S3 baseline (no skill), 2026-10-20

Short answer: look for the layer every agent payment must pass through that networks and Stripe are unlikely to build. Trust, liability and control are scarce; rails mostly exist.

Directions:
1. **Delegated authority / mandates** — neutral layer that issues, checks and audits mandates across AP2, Visa Intelligent Commerce, Mastercard Agent Pay (needs source). "OAuth for spending."
2. **Liability and disputes for agent-initiated transactions** — evidence trail, dispute handling, possibly insurance; chargeback rules assume a human clicked. Least crowded, hardest, possible system of record.
3. **Merchant-side agent acceptance** — score agent identity at checkout; per-transaction fee. Cloudflare and Visa moving (needs source).
4. **Spend controls for companies deploying agents** — per-task virtual cards, policies; crowded (Ramp, Brex, Lithic, Stripe Issuing, Payman, Skyfire; needs source).
5. **Machine-to-machine micropayments** — x402 etc.; tiny volume today (needs source).
6. **Compliance at agent speed** — KYC/AML, sanctions, travel rule for agent-run accounts.

Filters: will Visa/Mastercard/Stripe build it; does money move through it today; toll or system of record vs dashboard; licensing needed.

Next month: talk to 15–20 people who own risk or payments at companies letting agents spend; ask what stops them raising the agent's limit.

Sharpen: backgrounds; B2B or consumer; regulated flows or software-only. Then narrow to 2–3 bets with for/against.
