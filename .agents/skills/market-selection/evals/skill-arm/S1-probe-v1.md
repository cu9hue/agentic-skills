# S1 skill arm, 2026-10-20

Good moment to widen rather than narrow. Before I map anything, I need four short answers. All four fit in one reply.

**1. The bar.** Venture scale or bootstrap? Venture: which ceiling — $1bn ARR in under 10 years, a $1bn exit, or your own number? Bootstrap: roughly what income, by when?

**2. Your edge.** What can you build or do that few founders can? Compare to other founders, not colleagues. I'll restate it as capabilities that travel.

**3. Constraints.** Solo or team? B2B or consumer? Geography? Buyers you won't serve? Runway?

**4. What you've already explored.** Ideas tried or killed, and why. I won't re-screen them; I'll look for what they share.

While you answer, here's where I'd start looking — dated shifts, things more true this year than last:
- **Rules with deadlines** (2026–27) in areas you know (needs source).
- **Cost collapses** that open buyers who could never afford the service (needs source).
- **Incumbents retreating or repricing** — migration threads, two- and three-star reviews.

Then: 8–12 shifts, recurring patterns, 3–6 ranked directions, each with a belief and a cheap test against real buyers (e.g. 100 targeted messages over 2 weeks with a pass mark). Say "go" and I'll assume a bar and label it.
